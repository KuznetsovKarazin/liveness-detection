# M2, first tranche: NUAA manifests and overlap report

Generated by `scripts/nuaa_manifests.py report` from `nuaa_manifests_summary.json`, `overlap_report.json` and
`overlap_self_test.json` (commit `36c677aaa937d2997f727188878f1080706d12d3`, built 2026-10-06T20:07:05+0200). Public version: no subject identifiers and no per-image data. No inference and no training: the scripts
list and hash files and read the training cache (subject, label and frame arrays; the frames are only hashed).

## Source

- Dataset: NUAA Photograph Imposter Database (Tan, Li, Liu, Jiang, ECCV 2010), from the Hugging Face dataset akahana/anti-spoofing-nuaaaa,
  revision `6dd1ec237710732f92e73ca7653e695fd47c7a86`; archive `nuaaaa.tar.gz`, SHA-256 `c0780c6f1e581b720eb3c48faaeef7713748b174233d3fcafa8da716b5c81a3e` (equal to the etag recorded by the
  Hugging Face download cache).
- Field order: `ID_glasses_pos_session_picNo`, as stated on the M2 task card (3 October 2026), where the coordinator
  took it from the README of the original release; that README is not in the mirror and was not available to us. The
  reference publication of the database is Tan et al., ECCV 2010. On the data we verified the structure (five numeric
  fields), that ID equals the subject folder, and that the session field separates the official splits (01–02 train,
  03 test); the meaning of the glasses and pos codes is not verified and they are carried as opaque codes. Details:
  `naming_schema.md`.
- Split lists: the mirror's lists carry the path prefix `/kaggle/input/nuaaaa/raw/`, added by the mirror author: they
  are the mirror's version of the official lists. Only file names are compared. We did not verify their equivalence
  with the lists of the original release; the coordinator rebuilt the 300 C1 images from the official source with
  identical hashes (M1 card, 2 October 2026). `build` accepts only the mirror's list format.

Reconstruction of the training split: `spread(sorted file list, 1500)` per class and
`GroupShuffleSplit(test_size=0.2, random_state=42)` on the subject prefix, as in the training script. Subject and
label arrays equal to the training cache (`data/processed/nuaa_1500.npz`): train yes, test
yes. Independently of the scikit-learn version, among the 36 pairs of the 9 training subjects only one gives 493 validation images, the count recorded in the cards; the reconstruction is also checked against the subject arrays of the training cache. Build checks: 37, all passed: yes.

Validation images (`cnn_es`, 493): never used for gradient updates; their `val_loss` controlled early stopping (patience 4, best weights restored), hence which epoch's weights were saved, and the learning-rate reduction (ReduceLROnPlateau, factor 0.5, patience 2).

## Derived frames

The derived artefact of the images is the training cache: the preprocessed frames of the 3,000 sampled training
images and of the 3,000 test images. Each frame is hashed as stored (array bytes with dtype and shape); duplicates are
searched inside each array, between the two arrays and between the fitting and the validation part of the training
array (indices of the reconstruction).

- train frames: 3,000 (3,000 distinct, 0 duplicated values)
- test frames: 3,000 (3,000 distinct, 0 duplicated values)
- frames shared between train and test: 0; between the fitting and the validation images of the training split: 0

The frames carry no file name: they are linked to the image files through the sampling order and the subject and label
arrays, verified above, not image by image.

No image is shared, by key or by content hash, between any training-side manifest (official train, CNN train, validation, M1 dev) and any test-side manifest (official test, CNN test, C1), and no (subject, session) pair is shared. Subjects are shared: NUAA is not subject-disjoint between the official train and test splits.

Duplicates are exact (file SHA-256, or frame array bytes); near-duplicates (consecutive frames, the same printed
photograph) are not searched. This tranche does not establish absence of leakage. The (subject, session) rule between
train and test holds by construction of the NUAA protocol (sessions 01–02 vs 03); it is kept for the other datasets.

## Declarations

- 493 of the 574 M1 dev images are the validation images (`cnn_es`) of the four CNNs. The M1 dev set is therefore not independent of the training run.
- 22 C1 test images (22 attacks) come from the two validation subjects of the CNN training;
  they were captured in session 03, while the dev images of those subjects are from sessions 01 and 02.

## Supported comparisons (NUAA)

- Supported: a descriptive comparison of the four official CNNs trained on the same split, on the official protocol (disjoint by session, not by subject), single seed, without an uncertainty estimate; per-class error rates also reflect the class-dependent share of unseen subjects (above).
- Not supported: generalisation to subjects never seen in training; the M1 calibration is exploratory (validation
  images reused, two subjects); C1 is a subset of the test images. The NUAA pooled checkpoints are not covered (they are excluded from the C1 NUAA table because their random split shares subjects and sessions with the test images).

## Not reconstructible or not recorded

- The training run did not save its file list. The list is rebuilt from the sorted directory listing with the same
  sampling and split code and verified against the card counts and the subject/label arrays of the training cache.
- The training cache (`nuaa_1500.npz`, the derived artefact of the images) stores preprocessed arrays and subject
  prefixes, not paths or hashes: it is linked to the manifests by the group and label arrays in sampling order, not
  image by image; its frames are checked for duplicates by hash, not traced back to individual files.
- The four CNNs were trained on 12/09 with an earlier version of `scripts/train_cnn.py` (same preprocessing and
  hyper-parameters according to `research/c1-livenesslab/checkpoints/PROVENANCE.md`); the cards do not record the
  best epoch.
- The README of the original release is not in the mirror and was not available to us (see Source).
- The cards record the SHA-256 of the `.h5` only. For two of the four (`LivenessNet__nuaa`, `AttackNetV2_2__nuaa`) the
  `.h5` was exported from the `.keras` and found identical tensor by tensor (C1 `checkpoints/PROVENANCE.md`); the
  `.keras` files are not used.
- Subjects are the 4-digit IDs of the official folders and file names. No identity is inferred from image content or
  from groups of images. Treating the same ID in ClientRaw and ImposterRaw as the same person follows the naming of the
  release and is not verified on the images.
- NUAA has no video identifiers: frames are grouped only by subject and session (and picture number), so consecutive
  frames of a session are not independent samples.
