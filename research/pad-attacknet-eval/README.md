**The six runs** are recorded in the configuration: before the runs the field `runs` was `RUNS_PENDING`, and `evaluate`
refuses that form. After the six runs, `make_config.py --runs-root results/anetrun --force` writes, for each run, the run id of its last
attempt in the runs root and the SHA-256 of its `status.json` and of its two `SHA256SUMS`, read from the files, and
stops if any other field differs from the reference configuration: that of the last commit (`git show HEAD`), or the
file given with `--reference <file>` (the configuration of the published head of this pull request, saved with `git show
<commit>:delivery/aneteval/pad_anet_eval.json`). The comparison guarantees that only `runs` changed since the
reference, not that the reference was published. The completed configuration is committed, the package completed (configuration, logs, `SHA256SUMS`)
and then **published** (a commit of this pull request or a new pull request) **and linked

`SHA256SUMS`, and reads the not evaluable models with their reason from `status.json`.

## What `evaluate` does, in order

1. Before any folder: configuration, hooks (all `None` in an official command), pinned files, machine, versions and
   threads against the C5 trial, output root, the six runs, clean tree, attempt rule; then, with the manifests loaded,
   the records of the runs in this package (as `check-inputs`, below).
2. The dev scores of the evaluable new models against the manifests, their thresholds (0.5 and the dev EER threshold,
   from the saved dev scores, as in C2), the evaluation plan; then **the C2 gate**: from the 48 C2 test score files (each
   equal to the anchored restricted `SHA256SUMS`) the primary analysis, the 8 conditions and `units.csv` of C2 are
   recomputed with the frozen C2 functions and compared for exact equality with the published `results.json` and
   `units.csv` (+0.0998 [0.0256, 0.1632], 926 valid resamples). A difference: FAIL, before any test frame of the new
   models. Then the EER thresholds of the 48 files compared with `models[].dev_eer_threshold` of the C2 `results.json`
   (`PROTOCOL.md` §4), and the descriptive comparison of LivenessNet with ResNet-18, computed on the C2 units alone,
   checked to be the exact opposite of the C2 gaps and contrast. `check-inputs` makes the same checks.
3. TensorFlow (10 + 10 threads, CPU only); each evaluable new model reloaded, checked against the registry and its dev
   reproduced in both BN states against the saved dev scores (tolerances of PR #27); one model out: MISMATCH, the test is
   not read.
4. Re-verification; `test_read_started` recorded (`status.json` rewritten, still RUNNING, with `test_read_started`
   true); the fold schema; the frame identifiers of the 48 C2 files compared with the test keys of their fold; per fold the test frames assembled once and scored by its new models in both BN
   states, the per-frame scores of each model written to `restricted/` as soon as computed. A model with non-finite test
   scores: NON_FINITE_TEST with the count of such frames, the restricted scores kept with their `SHA256SUMS`, no analysis
   (`PROTOCOL.md` §6 (n)).
5. Re-verification, the units of the 8 conditions (the new AttackNets and the 48 C2 references on the same test frames),
   `pad_anet_definitions.primary_analysis` and a declared copy of `pad_anet_definitions.descriptive_analysis` whose only
   difference is the reasons of the not evaluable models per patience (with the guardian per network: an AttackNet with
   fewer than 12 pairs evaluable in both BN states is "not computed" with the reason of its not evaluable models), the
   check that the descriptive comparison of LivenessNet with ResNet-18 is the exact opposite of the C2 gaps and
   contrast (again, after the analysis), the count of `PROTOCOL.md` §6 (f) on the test, the outputs. No model is excluded
   after its test scores are computed.

**After a stop.** No automatic resume. `test_read_started` is recorded in the `status.json` of every attempt: false at
the start, rewritten true before the fold schema and any test frame, so that an abrupt stop after that point (battery,
forced quit, memory) leaves a RUNNING `status.json` with `test_read_started` true. After the reading of the test the
restricted scores already computed are kept, with their `SHA256SUMS`, also for FAIL and INCOMPLETE. A new attempt after
an attempt that started reading the test, or that was left RUNNING, or whose `restricted/` is not empty, or whose status
cannot be read, is a second reading of the test: refused unless `--second-reading`, given only after the outcome has
been reported on the C5 card, and recorded in `status.json` and `results.json`. A second reading is launched by hand
with `--second-reading`, never by the launch script, which does not pass that option.

Outcomes: PASS 0, FAIL 1, INCOMPLETE 2 (cap of 2.0 hours of wall time or SIGUSR1), MISMATCH 4, NON_FINITE_TEST 6.

Outputs in `results/aneteval/YYYYMMDD-ANETEVAL-<commit>[-r<k>]/`: `status.json`, `run.log`; `public/` `results.json`
(primary analysis, descriptive conditions, C2 reproduction, not evaluable models, and `test_one_class`: per network ×
patience × BN state × threshold, the models deciding one class for every test frame, `PROTOCOL.md` §6 (f)), `report.md`, `table.csv` (the five
networks at patience 4 and threshold 0.5 in both BN states: the mean over the units of the per-unit APCER, BPCER and
ACER, with the number of units of the network and its denominators summed over those units; the denominators per fold
are in `results.json`, `primary_analysis.denominators`), `conditions.csv` (variations and contrasts of the four pairs of conditions), `units.csv` (per condition, unit and network
the threshold and the exact APCER, BPCER and ACER: the per-network means of the 8 conditions follow from it, without
reading the test again), `reproduction.csv`, `SHA256SUMS` (counts and SHA-256, no subject or frame
identifier); `restricted/` the per-frame test scores of each new model and `SHA256SUMS`, not published.

## The records of the runs

As for C2 (`research/pad-arch-eval/runs/`), this folder publishes in `runs/<run id>/` the records of the commands of the
runs: for every attempt of each of the six runs and for every check-estimator of the runs root, `status.json`,
`run.log` and `public/` (nothing of `restricted/`), with `conditions.txt`, the conditions file written by
`delivery/anetrun/launch.zsh` at the launch (its first line is the heading written by that script), copied unchanged.
They are copied by `delivery/aneteval/copy_run_records.py --runs-root results/anetrun --conditions-dir <folder of the
launch logs>`, which refuses a missing conditions file or a text a publishable output must not hold; `check-inputs` and
`evaluate` check that the package holds exactly those folders and files, each byte for byte equal to the file of the
runs root, and scan every copy. The scan of `copy_run_records.py` and `check-inputs` does not use the manifests: it
finds personal paths and texts, not subject or frame identifiers; `evaluate` scans with the manifests loaded. For
`run.log` and `public/` the scan repeats that of the runner, made with the real inputs; `conditions.txt` holds only the
outputs of `check-machine` and `check-inputs`.

## Commands, after the six runs

```
.venv/bin/python -I -B delivery/aneteval/copy_run_records.py --runs-root results/anetrun --conditions-dir <folder of the launch logs>
.venv/bin/python -I -B delivery/aneteval/make_config.py --runs-root results/anetrun --force
setopt nobgnice
ANETEVAL_LOG_DIR=<a folder outside the repository> zsh delivery/aneteval/launch.zsh check-inputs
ANETEVAL_LOG_DIR=<a folder outside the repository> ANETEVAL_ATTEMPT=1 nohup zsh delivery/aneteval/launch.zsh evaluate > <a log file outside the repository> 2>&1 &
```

with a commit after the first two commands (and the publication and link of the completed configuration, above) and
after `evaluate`. The launch script (as `delivery/anetrun/launch.zsh` for the runs) stops without launching the command
if it is not run from the root of the repository, if `ANETEVAL_LOG_DIR` is missing or inside the repository, if the
command is neither `check-inputs` nor `evaluate`, if `evaluate` has no `ANETEVAL_ATTEMPT`, or if the conditions file of
the same label already exists (exit 9); it also stops, with the reason in the conditions file, if its nice value is
not 0, if `pmset -g batt` does not say "AC Power" (`evaluate` only), or if `check-machine` of the runs or `check-inputs`
of the evaluation fails (exit 1); otherwise it runs the command under `caffeinate -dims`, propagates its exit code, and writes in the log folder
`eval-check-inputs-<YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS>-conditions.txt` or `evaluate-attempt<N>-conditions.txt` (date, load, CPU usage
and power source at the start and at the end, `sw_vers`, power mode from `pmset`, the nice value, the number of `TF_NUM_` variables, commit and lines of `git status
--porcelain`, the outputs of both checks, the command and its exit code). The C2 evaluation had no conditions file; this
one is published with the record of the evaluation. The launch script never passes `--second-reading`: a second
reading, if ever needed, is launched by hand with `--second-reading` under `after_stop` (above).
